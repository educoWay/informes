Universidades “Research” vs “Applied Sciences”

In most European countries we have to differentiate between 2 types of universities: "Research" and "Applied Sciences" The two types of university share many attractive features, but to fully understand these two systems, it is helpful to look at their main differences:

· Theoretical and practical methodology

The universities of Applied Sciences They stand out for using a very practical methodology called PBL (Project/Problem Based Learning The aim is to simulate a real-world, project-based work environment from the first year, where students collaborate with colleagues, research to find and evaluate solutions, and reach a consensus on a strategy that must then be implemented together. It's a fascinating methodology (the same one used in Denmark) that greatly facilitates learning and the acquisition of real-world skills that you can apply as soon as you graduate. These types of universities almost always include a mandatory one-semester professional internship. Universities in Research They don't have such a practical approach; instead, they try to lay a solid foundation in theory and research skills so that students can then choose a master's degree in which to specialize and where they acquire more practical skills (more applicable to their job). These universities don't usually include internships as part of the degree program.

· Duration

The universities of Applied Sciences They last one year longer (4 years, 240 ECTS) because they include internships and are designed to allow you to enter the job market directly without going through a master's degree.

The ones of Research They only last 3 years (180 ECTS) because they do not have internships and are usually complemented with a 1 or 2 year master's degree.

· Ranking / Prestige

The main reason why one might choose a university in Research It is because of the higher ranking and prestige that these universities have.

The universities of Applied Sciences (University of Applied Sciences (in Dutch) are sometimes undervalued among students because they do not appear in international rankings (they do not have research faculties that develop studies or scientific publications, which is what scores the most in the rankings).

· Access to Master's Degrees

Here it's important to differentiate whether your master's degree will be in the Netherlands or another country. If it's outside the Netherlands, both degrees offer you the same opportunities. If it's in the Netherlands, with your university degree from Research You will be able to access master's degrees at universities in Research how Applied Sciences However, with your title of Applied Sciences You will be able to access master's degrees at universities in Applied Sciences But if you want to access universities in Research, You will be required to complete a pre-master's course (lasting 1 semester or one year).

Your Bachelor's thesis will take up a grand part of the first half of your third year.
